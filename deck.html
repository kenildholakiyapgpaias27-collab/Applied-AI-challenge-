✦ Melanin-Aware Safety Rules

Why Skin Tone Dictates Product Safety

✦ CLICK ANY SHADE BELOW
GOOGLE MONK SCALE:
MST 07 · Fitzpatrick Type V (Melanocompetent)

In dermatological medicine, skin tone dictates biological reactivity. Darker complexions feature hyper-reactive basal melanocytes that trigger stubborn hyperpigmentation when exposed to harsh chemicals.

SAFETY CONSTRAINT 01 · PIH RISK

Post-Inflammatory Hyperpigmentation

On melanocompetent skin (Monk 5–10), aggressive chemical peels or unbuffered high-strength acids trigger trauma in basal melanocytes, causing dark spots that take up to a year to fade.

AAINA Rule: Hard ceiling on unbuffered Glycolic Acid (≤5%) and Salicylic Acid (≤2%). Automatically favors gentle Niacinamide and Azelaic Acid.
SAFETY CONSTRAINT 02 · OPTICAL CAST

Sunscreen White-Cast Penalty

Pure physical sunscreens (Zinc Oxide and Titanium Dioxide) scatter visible light. On deeper complexions, this leaves an unsightly purple-gray chalky film, causing users to skip daily sunscreen.

AAINA Rule: Automatically penalizes pure mineral zinc formulations on Monk 6–10 and recommends invisible hybrid or photostable chemical UV filters.
